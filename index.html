Privacy Policy – LostDrops

Data Storage
Your stage progress, best move counts, and settings are stored locally on your device. LostDrops does not require an account or upload your progress to a remote database. Deleting the app may remove this locally stored data.

Data Collection & Sharing
LostDrops does not collect personal information or use third-party analytics or advertising services in this release. We do not sell, trade, or share personal user data.

Support & External Links
If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. Links to external websites open services governed by their own privacy policies.

Privacy Policy – MyScorePaper

MyScorePaper does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers. All scores, player names, and game history remain stored locally on the device unless explicitly stated within the app.

Advertising
The free version of MyScorePaper uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect anonymized device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

MyScorePaper does not require account creation and does not sell or share user data.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers.
